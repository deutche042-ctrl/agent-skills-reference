# Original Script
I am timid and cowardly, but I am bound to a system that makes lies come true.
On our wedding night, my husband Lu Yanli looked troubled.
“Wanyu, I was injured in battle and lost my manhood. Marrying me has truly wronged you.”
“But I swear that, apart from children, I will give you everything I can.”
The system notification sounded.
【Beep! Young Marquis Lu Yanli's manhood has been destroyed.】
I could not help widening my eyes.
Was the young marquis really taking it this far?
The instant the system notification ended, Lu Yanli's agonized wail rang beside me.
Clutching his groin, he cried without pause: “Physician! Send for the physician!”
I looked troubled. “Has my husband's old injury returned? Calling a physician on our wedding day...”
I glanced at the place he was clutching. “And for an injury there, I fear the capital will be full of gossip tomorrow. How will the marquis socialize afterward?”
Lu Yanli grimaced in such pain that he ignored my advice and shouted only for the physician.
“Physician! I am nearly dying of pain—can you not see?”
Fine, fine. I am kindhearted and timid.
How could a bride who had just entered the household dare make the decision?
So I could only go tearfully with my nursemaid to beg the old marchioness in her room.
The old marchioness's eyes darted about as she frowned. “Wanyu, Yanli has lost his head from the pain. Why can you not make up your mind either?”
“It is an old ailment there. If it hurts, he can endure it. What else can be done?”
“Or are you shameless? Having learned that your husband is impotent, do you mean to cause a public scene and make the whole world laugh at our household?”
Heaven is my witness.
I am timid and afraid of trouble.
When my half-sister cried that her body was cold and asked for the warm jade my uncle gave me, I could only hand it over, however unwillingly.
No sooner had she taken it than the system sounded: 【Beep! Cold disease has invaded Shen Wanqiu; she will never bear children.】
I was too timid to make trouble and could only pray that the warm jade truly helped her cold constitution.
The dog I had raised since childhood died after my half-brother accidentally fed it arsenic. He cried even more pitifully than I did and said he was foolish.
What could I do? I could only bury the dog in tears and dared not say a word.
But from that day forward, my half-brother truly became foolish.
He could not take the imperial examinations; he could not even care for himself.
I had walked on eggshells in my maiden home, terrified that anyone would discover my secret.
Now I had married into the marquis's household. The marquis was irritable from his illness, while the old marchioness prized appearances and refused to make the matter public.
Aggrieved, I could only take my nursemaid to spend the night in the small side room.
After all, I dared offend neither my mother-in-law nor the marquis.
The only problem was that the marquis's cries were terrifying.
They continued intermittently all night.

## Format Instructions

### 1. Overall Structure
- Output sections in this exact order: Original Script → Character Profiles → Character Prompts → Sora Storyboard Prompts → Scene Prompts → Shot Prompts.
- Use level-one headings (`#`) to separate sections.
- Leave blank lines between sections.
- Separate character profiles with three asterisks (`***`).

### 2. Character-Profile Format
- Fields: Character name, Appearance, Occupation/identity, Personality, Character biography.
- Put each field on its own line in `Field name: value` format.
- The biography may contain paragraphs but must not use headings.

### 3. Character-Prompt Format
- List each character's prompt directly.
- Format: `Character name: [prompt content]`.
- Give each character a separate paragraph.

### 4. Sora Storyboard-Prompt Format
- Use a fenced `json` code block.
- Use a JSON array with one storyboard object per element.
- Required fields: `storyboard_number`, `sora_prompt`.
- Within `sora_prompt`, label time ranges as `[X–Y seconds]`.
- Each time range includes: Shot, Sound/music, Emotion/atmosphere, Narration/dialogue.

### 5. Scene-Prompt Format
- Format: `Scene N: [scene name] — [scene subtitle]`.
- Two required fields: Visual description and Image-generation prompt.
- Give each scene a separate paragraph.

### 6. Shot-Prompt Format
- Use a Markdown table.
- Columns: Shot, Scene, Shot Size, Shot Content, Duration, Camera Movement, Sound / Dialogue, Notes (optimized for 即梦 generation).
- Put one shot in each row.

### 7. Punctuation
- Use English punctuation in English content.
- Preserve platform-specific literals such as `即梦` exactly as written.
- Preserve the exact syntax of code blocks.
- Use a colon after field names.
