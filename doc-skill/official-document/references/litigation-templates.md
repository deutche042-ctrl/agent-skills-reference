# Collection of Litigation-Document Templates

Based on the Supreme People's Court's "Formats of Civil Litigation Documents" (2024 revision) and "Specifications for Preparing People's Court Civil Adjudication Documents."

## Statement of Defense

```
CIVIL STATEMENT OF DEFENSE

Respondent: [Name/Entity name], [basic information in the same format as the complaint]

In the case of [dispute over cause of action] brought by [plaintiff's name/entity name]
against the respondent (case number: [case number]), the respondent now
submits the following defense:

1. Defense to the plaintiff's first claim for relief
[Defend against each of the plaintiff's claims in turn, stating the facts and legal basis]

2. Defense to the plaintiff's second claim for relief
[Same as above]

3. [Supplementary defense opinions]

In summary, the plaintiff's claims for relief lack a factual and legal basis;
the court is respectfully requested to dismiss all/part of the plaintiff's claims for relief in accordance with the law.

List of Evidence:
1. [Name of evidence]—to prove [fact to be proven]
2. [Name of evidence]—to prove [fact to be proven]

Respectfully submitted to
[Full name of the court]

                                    Respondent: [Signature/Seal]
                                    [Year] Year [Month] Month [Day] Day
```

## Notice of Appeal

```
CIVIL NOTICE OF APPEAL

Appellant (plaintiff/defendant in the original trial): [Name/Entity name], [basic information]
Appellee (defendant/plaintiff in the original trial): [Name/Entity name], [basic information]

In the case of a dispute over [cause of action] between the appellant and the appellee,
the appellant refuses to accept the (case number: [case number]) civil judgment
rendered by [full name of the court of first instance] on [Year] Year [Month] Month [Day] Day,
and now files this appeal.

Appeal Requests:
1. Set aside item [X] of the original judgment;
2. Revise the judgment in accordance with the law to [specific request] (or remand for retrial);
3. The litigation costs of the [first instance/second instance] shall be borne by the appellee.

Grounds of Appeal:
1. The original trial's findings of fact were erroneous
[Specific elaboration]

2. The original trial applied the law erroneously
[Specific elaboration]

3. [Other grounds]

Respectfully submitted to
[Full name of the court at the next higher level]

                                    Appellant: [Signature/Seal]
                                    [Year] Year [Month] Month [Day] Day

Attached: [X] copies of this notice of appeal
```

## Application for Property Preservation

```
APPLICATION FOR PROPERTY PRESERVATION

Applicant: [Name/Entity name], [basic information]
Respondent: [Name/Entity name], [basic information]

Matters Requested:
The applicant requests that preservation measures be taken against property
in the respondent's name valued at RMB [amount],
including but not limited to: [bank deposits/real estate/vehicles/equity, etc.].

Facts and Reasons:
Due to a dispute over [cause of action] between the applicant and the respondent, [brief description of the situation].
Because the respondent is engaged in [transferring property/maliciously disposing of property, etc.],
failure to take preservation measures in a timely manner would cause the applicant's
lawful rights and interests to suffer irreparable harm.

Pursuant to Articles 103 and 104 of the Civil Procedure Law of the People's Republic of China,
the applicant hereby applies to your court for property preservation.

The applicant is willing to provide security in accordance with the law.

Respectfully submitted to
[Full name of the court]

                                    Applicant: [Signature/Seal]
                                    [Year] Year [Month] Month [Day] Day
```

## Claim-for-Relief Patterns for Common Causes of Action

| Cause of action | Typical wording of claims for relief |
|---|---|
| Sales-contract dispute | Order the defendant to pay goods payment of RMB XX and overdue interest (from Month X, Day X, Year X until the date of actual payment, calculated at the LPR) |
| Private-lending dispute | Order the defendant to repay the loan principal of RMB XX and interest (calculated at an annual interest rate of X%) |
| Labor dispute | Order the defendant to pay economic compensation/damages of RMB XX; make up the social insurance from Month X, Year X to Month X, Year X |
| Housing-lease dispute | Order the defendant to vacate the house located at [address] and pay overdue rent of RMB XX and occupancy fees |
| Motor-vehicle traffic accident | Order the defendant to compensate medical expenses of RMB XX, lost-work expenses of RMB XX, and disability compensation of RMB XX, totaling RMB XX |
| Divorce dispute | Grant the divorce of the plaintiff and the defendant; the child born in wedlock shall be raised by the plaintiff, and the defendant shall pay child support of RMB XX per month; divide the marital community property in accordance with the law |

## Document Language Conventions

- Use "plaintiff" and "defendant" rather than "I" and "the other party"
- State amounts in both words and figures: `RMB Ten Thousand Yuan only (¥10,000.00)`
- Date format: `二〇二六年三月十日` (the tenth day of March, 2026) or `2026年3月10日` (March 10, 2026)
- Legal-citation format: `Article 577 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China`
- Avoid emotional expressions; remain objective and neutral
