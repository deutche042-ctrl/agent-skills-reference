# Japan Visa Application Guide（査証申請ガイド）

Form structure, field descriptions, and support-material specifications for Chinese citizens applying for a Japan short-term stay visa (tourism/business/visiting relatives).

---

## 1. Visa Application Form (査証申請書) Structure

The Japan visa application form is a **paper form** (PDF), 2 pages long, downloadable from the website of the Embassy/Consulate General of Japan in China. Chinese citizens must submit their application through a designated travel agency/authorized agent.

### Part 1: Applicant Basic Information (Items 1–10)

| No. | Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Field (Japanese) | Instructions |
|------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| 1 | 姓 | Surname | 姓 | Chinese + English (e.g., 张 / ZHANG) |
| 2 | 名 | Given Name | 名 | Chinese + English (e.g., 三 / SAN) |
| 3 | 曾用名 | Former Name | 旧姓・旧名 | N/A if none |
| 4 | 出生日期 | Date of Birth | 生年月日 | **DD.MMM.YYYY** (e.g., 14.DEC.1988) |
| 5 | 出生地 | Place of Birth | 出生地 | City name (e.g., 上海 / SHANGHAI) |
| 6 | 性别 | Sex | 性別 | Male / Female |
| 7 | 婚姻状况 | Marital Status | 配偶の有無 | Single / Married / Widowed / Divorced |
| 8 | 国籍 | Nationality | 国籍 | CHINESE |
| 9 | 曾持有国籍 | Former Nationality | 以前の国籍 | N/A if none |
| 10 | 身份证号 | National ID No. | 国民登録番号 | 18-digit national ID number |

### Part 2: Passport Information (Items 11–16)

| No. | Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Field (Japanese) | Instructions |
|------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| 11 | 护照类别 | Passport Type | 旅券の種類 | Diplomatic / Official / Ordinary / Other |
| 12 | 护照号码 | Passport No. | 旅券番号 | e.g., E12345678 |
| 13 | 签发地点 | Place of Issue | 発行地 | e.g., SHANGHAI |
| 14 | 签发日期 | Date of Issue | 発行年月日 | DD.MMM.YYYY (e.g., 02.MAR.2017) |
| 15 | 签发机关 | Issuing Authority | 発行官庁 | e.g., MPS Exit-Entry Administration |
| 16 | 有效期限 | Date of Expiry | 有効期限 | DD.MMM.YYYY |

### Part 3: Travel Information (Items 17–26)

| No. | Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Field (Japanese) | Instructions |
|------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| 17 | 赴日目的 | Purpose of Visit | 渡航目的 | Tourism / Business / Visiting Relatives, etc. |
| 18 | 预计停留时间 | Intended Length of Stay | 滞在予定期間 | e.g., 15 days |
| 19 | 入境日期 | Intended Date of Entry | 入国予定日 | YYYY/MM/DD |
| 19b | 出境日期 | Intended Date of Departure | 出国予定日 | YYYY/MM/DD |
| 20 | 入境口岸 | Port of Entry | 入国港 | e.g., Narita / Kansai / Haneda |
| 21 | 航班/船舶 | Name of Ship or Airline | 船舶/航空機名 | e.g., JAL-006 / ANA-921 |
| 22 | 在日住宿名称 | Name of Hotel/Host | 滞在先名称 | Hotel name or host's name |
| 23 | 在日住宿地址 | Address in Japan | 滞在先住所 | Full address |
| 24 | 在日联系电话 | Phone in Japan | 滞在先電話 | |
| 25 | 担保人/邀请人 | Guarantor/Inviter | 保証人/招聘者 | Name, address, phone, relationship to applicant |
| 26 | 上次赴日记录 | Previous Visit to Japan | 過去の渡航歴 | Dates and length of stay |

### Part 4: Contact Information (Items 27–29)

| No. | Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Instructions |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| 27 | 现住址 | Current Address | Address in China (Chinese and English) |
| 28 | 电话号码 | Phone Number | +86-XXX-XXXX-XXXX |
| 29 | 紧急联系人 | Emergency Contact | Name, phone, relationship |

### Security Declaration Section

You must answer whether you have any history of criminal record, illegal overstay, deportation, drug offenses, etc. Check Yes / No truthfully.

## 3. Date and Format Conventions

| Item | Japan Visa Format |
|------|------------|
| Date (form) | **DD.MMM.YYYY** (e.g., 14.DEC.1988) or **YYYY年MM月DD日** |
| Date (support materials) | **YYYY年MM月DD日** or **YYYY/MM/DD** |
| Name | Chinese + English side by side (e.g., 張三 / ZHANG SAN) |
| Phone | +86-138-0000-0000 |
| Currency | JPY (Japanese yen), e.g., JPY 500,000; write RMB as CNY or RMB |
| Address | Japanese addresses go from largest to smallest unit (都道府県 → 市区町村 → 番地); Chinese addresses in English go from smallest to largest |

| ... | ... | ... |
| MM/DD (Day N) | [Departure City] → China (flight number), return | — |

## 5. Special Notes for Japan Visas

1. **Applications must be submitted through a designated authorized agent** — individuals cannot submit directly to the consulate
2. **Guarantor/inviter**: for visiting relatives or friends, a tax payment certificate, residence certificate (住民票), etc. of the guarantor in Japan must be provided
3. **Three-year / five-year multiple-entry visas**: annual income requirements must be met (roughly ¥200,000+/year before tax for a three-year visa, ¥500,000+/year before tax for a five-year visa)
4. **Proof of financial capacity**: a bank statement balance of ≥ RMB 100,000 is recommended (single tourist trip)
5. All photocopies must be on **A4 paper**
6. Visa fee: approx. ¥200 (RMB) for single entry, approx. ¥400 for multiple entry
7. Processing time: approx. 5–7 business days
