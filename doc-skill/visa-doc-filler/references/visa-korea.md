# South Korea Visa Application Guide（비자 신청 가이드）

Form structure, field descriptions, and support-material specifications for Chinese citizens applying for a short-term South Korea visa (C-3 tourism/business).

---

## 1. Visa Application Form Structure

The South Korea visa application form is a standard format, downloadable in PDF / Word / HWP versions from the website of the Embassy of the Republic of Korea in China. The form is 4 pages long and must be printed on **A4 paper**.

### Basic Filling Requirements

- Fill in using **BLOCK LETTERS** (uppercase)
- Language: **English or Korean**
- Date format: **YYYY/MM/DD**
- Mark multiple-choice items with **[√]**
- Enter **N/A** for items that do not apply
- Filling in by computer is recommended (handwriting is also accepted)

## 5. Special Notes for South Korea Visas

1. **Form download**: download the latest version from the website of the Embassy of Korea in China, available in three formats — PDF / Word / HWP
2. **Chinese-character name** (한자성명): Chinese citizens must fill in their Chinese-character name
3. **Visa category codes**: C-3-9 for tourism, C-3-4 for business, C-3-1 for visiting relatives
4. Recommended proof of financial capacity: bank statement balance ≥ RMB 50,000
5. Employed applicants must provide a **photocopy of the business license (duplicate copy)**
6. Visa fee: approx. ¥280 (RMB) for single entry, approx. ¥490 for multiple entry
7. Processing time: approx. 5–7 business days
8. The photo must be affixed at the designated position on the first page of the application form
