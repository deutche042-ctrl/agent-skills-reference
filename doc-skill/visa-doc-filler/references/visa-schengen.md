# Schengen Visa Application Guide

Form structure, field descriptions, and support-material specifications for Chinese citizens applying for a short-term Schengen visa (tourism/business/visiting relatives). The Schengen visa is valid for the 26 member states (France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria, Belgium, Greece, Portugal, etc.).

---

## 1. Schengen Visa Application Form Structure

The Schengen visa uses a **uniform standard form** (Harmonised Application Form), common to all member states. Form reference cerfa No. 14076*02, based on Regulation (EC) No 810/2009 (EU Visa Code).

### Basic Filling Requirements

- May be handwritten (black or blue ink pen) or completed by computer and printed
- Print on **A4 paper** (double-sided is fine)
- Use **UPPERCASE LATIN LETTERS**
- Date format: **DD/MM/YYYY** or **DD.MM.YYYY**
- Mark multiple-choice items with **×**
- Enter **N/A** or **None** for items that do not apply (do not leave blank)
- The form must be redone if there are more than 4 errors

| No. | Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Instructions |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| 1 | 姓（家族名） | Surname (Family name) | Exactly as in passport, including prefixes such as DE/VAN |

| No. | Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Instructions |
|------|---------|---------|---------|

### Section 4: Trip Information (Items 20–26) ⚠️ Most Important Section

| No. | Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Instructions |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| 20 | 旅行目的 | Purpose of the journey | Tourism / Visiting family or friends / Business / Cultural / Sports / Study / Medical / Transit / Other |
| 21 | 首次入境成员国 | Member State of first entry | The first Schengen country you set foot in (even if only a transfer) |
| 22 | 申请入境次数 | Number of entries requested | Single / Double / Multiple |
| 23 | 停留天数 | Duration of stay | Total days (both the entry and departure days count as one day each) |
| 24 | 到达/离开日期 | Dates of arrival and departure | DD/MM/YYYY — DD/MM/YYYY |
| 25 | 目的地国家 | Destination country/countries | List all Schengen countries in order of visit |
| 26 | 预计到达/离开申根区日期 | Intended date of arrival/departure from Schengen | DD/MM/YYYY |

**Key rules:**
- Whichever country you stay in longest → apply to that country's embassy
- If the stay is equal across multiple countries → apply to the embassy of the country of first entry
- Dates must exactly match the flight tickets and hotel bookings

|------|---------|---------|---------|

| No. | Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Instructions |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| 32 | 是否有申根拒签/拒绝入境/驱逐记录 | Previous refusal / entry refusal / expulsion | **Must be answered truthfully**; the VIS system can cross-verify |

### Section 8: EU Family (Item 33)

Only filled in by non-EU citizens exercising freedom of movement while accompanying an EU family member.

### Section 9: Declaration and Signature

Confirm that the information is true and complete, and consent to data processing. Handwritten signature (black or blue pen).

## 5. Special Notes for Schengen Visas

1. **Travel insurance** (mandatory): coverage ≥ **EUR 30,000**, covering all Schengen countries and all travel dates, including medical repatriation
2. **Apply to the main destination country**: apply to the embassy of whichever country you stay in longest
3. **Multi-country itineraries**: the cover letter and itinerary must clearly indicate the number of days spent in each country
4. **Hotel bookings** must cover all accommodation dates, and the booking name must match the passport
5. **Bank statements**: a balance of ≥ 100 EUR × days of stay is recommended
6. Fingerprint collection: required for first-time applications or when fingerprints are older than 59 months
7. Visa fee: EUR 90 for adults (2026 standard), EUR 45 for ages 6–12
8. Processing time: usually 15 business days, up to 45 days in peak season
9. **Refusal records must be declared truthfully**; the VIS system is shared across all of Europe
