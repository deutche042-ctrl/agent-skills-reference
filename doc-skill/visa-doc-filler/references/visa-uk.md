# UK Visa Application Guide

Form structure, field descriptions, and support-material specifications for Chinese citizens applying for a UK Standard Visitor Visa.

---

## 1. Online Application Form Structure

The UK visa application is completed **entirely online** (gov.uk); there is **no downloadable blank PDF form**. After submission and payment, the system generates a PDF summary for download.

### The Seven Sections of the Form

#### Section 1: Travel Information

| Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Instructions |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 到达日期 | Date you will arrive in the UK | DD/MM/YYYY |
| 离开日期 | Date you will leave the UK | DD/MM/YYYY |
| 访问主要目的 | Main purpose of your visit | Tourism / Visiting family or friends / Business / Medical / Study / Other |
| 详细访问目的 | Give details of the main purpose of your visit and anything else you plan to do | A detailed 2–4 sentence description, consistent with the supporting documents |
| 是否团队出行 | Travelling as part of an organised group? | Yes / No |
| 同行人信息 | Travel companions | Name, relationship, passport number |

**Sample wording for "detailed purpose of visit":**

| Type | Example |
|------|-----|
| Tourism | "Holiday and sightseeing in London from 15-29 July 2026. Plan to visit Tower of London, British Museum, with day trips to Oxford and Bath. Detailed itinerary attached." |
| Visiting family | "Visiting my daughter [Name] and her family in Manchester from 1-21 August 2026 for family reunion. I will stay at their home." |
| Business | "Attending business meetings with ABC Ltd in London from 5-9 March 2026. Invitation letter attached. No paid work will be undertaken." |

| Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Instructions |

**Travel budget reference:**

| Trip | Budget range (GBP) |
|------|---------------|
| 1 week, individual | £800-£2,000 |
| 2 weeks, individual | £1,500-£3,500 |
| 2 weeks, couple | £2,500-£6,000 |
| 2 weeks, family (4–5 people) | £5,000-£15,000 |

#### Section 5: Travel History

| Field (Chinese) | Field (English) | Instructions |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 近10年英国旅行记录 | UK travel in the last 10 years | Dates, duration, purpose |
| 近10年赴美/加/澳/新/申根记录 | Travel to US/Canada/Australia/NZ/Schengen | Same as above |
| 是否有拒签记录 | Have you ever been refused a visa for any country | **Must be answered truthfully**; concealment can lead to a 10-year ban |
| 是否被遣返/驱逐 | Deportation or removal history | Yes / No |

#### Section 6: Other History, Convictions and Medical

Covers criminal records, war crimes, terrorism connections, military/government service history, whether you have received medical treatment in the UK, etc.

#### Section 7: Extra Information and Declaration

You may add explanations of special circumstances (e.g., employment gaps, reasons for a previous refusal, etc.). Confirm that the information is true and sign the declaration.

## 2. Photo Requirements

UK visa photos are taken on the spot at the biometrics appointment, so you usually do not need to provide your own. But prepare a spare:

| Item | Requirement |
|------|------|
| Size | 45mm × 35mm |
| Background | Light gray or white |
| Taken within | The last 6 months |

## 5. Special Notes for UK Visas

1. **Entirely online application**, no paper form to download; print the PDF summary after completion
2. **Biometrics collection**: you must book an appointment at a visa center for on-site fingerprinting and photography
3. **GWF number**: after submitting the application you receive a 12-character alphanumeric number (e.g., GWF041163715), used to track progress
4. **Travel history is very important**: UK/US/Canada/Australia/NZ/Schengen travel in the last 10 years must be reported truthfully
5. **Refusal records must be declared truthfully**: concealment can result in a **10-year ban from entering the UK**
6. Visa fee: Standard Visitor Visa (6 months) £127, long-term (2 years) £475, (5 years) £848, (10 years) £1,059
7. Priority service: +£500 (5 business days), super priority: +£1,000 (next business day)
8. Standard processing time: approximately 3 weeks
9. **The UK is not part of the Schengen Area**; a separate visa is required
10. Supporting document language: English; Chinese documents must be accompanied by an English translation
