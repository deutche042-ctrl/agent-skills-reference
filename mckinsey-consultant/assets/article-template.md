# Long-Form Article Structure Template

## Instructions
Use this template to create a long-form article based on the Pyramid Principle, with a clear structure and rigorous logic. Fill each section with the relevant content.

---

## Title
**Requirement**: Make it compelling, include the core benefit, and summarize the article's central value.

**Examples**:
- *The Remote-Work Productivity Drain: A Structural Shift from Physical Distance to Collaboration Mechanisms*
- *The Key to Implementing AI: From Technical Sophistication to Contextual Fit*
- *The Age-35 Career Crisis: A Value-Positioning Mismatch, Not Declining Capability*

---

## Introduction (SCQA)
**Requirement**: Use the SCQA framework to open with a story and quickly capture the reader's attention.

### S — Situation
Describe background facts about the current state that everyone accepts in one or two sentences.

**Example**:
Remote work has become mainstream over the past three years, and companies have widely adopted flexible-work policies.

### C — Complication
Identify a current pain point, change, or counterintuitive phenomenon in one or two sentences.

**Example**:
However, surveys show that 70% of employees report a significant decline in productivity after moving to remote work, while the quality of team collaboration has also suffered.

### Q — Question
Distill the central question the article will answer in one sentence.

**Example**:
What is the real cause of lower remote-work productivity?

### A — Answer (Core Conclusion)
State an insightful central point based on professional analysis in one or two sentences.

**Example**:
The primary cause of lower remote-work productivity is the lack of structured collaboration mechanisms, not physical distance itself.

## Body (Develop the Key Arguments)
**Requirement**: Use the format “subheading + topic sentence + detailed explanation.” Each argument should include an in-depth explanation, case evidence, and actionable recommendations.

### Argument One: [Subheading]
**Topic sentence**: Summarize the argument's central point in one sentence.

**Detailed explanation**:
1. **Phenomenon**: Specific manifestations and supporting data
2. **Deep analysis**: The mechanism and reasons behind the phenomenon—why it occurs
3. **Case evidence**: Specific cases, data, and research citations
4. **Recommendation**: An actionable recommendation—what to do

**Example structure**:
> **Synchronous Communication Becomes Less Efficient**
>
> In remote-work environments, 75% of employees report that meetings have become significantly less efficient. This is not a technology problem but a lack of real-time, synchronous communication mechanisms. When team members are distributed across time zones or physical locations, delays in information transfer lengthen decision cycles.
>
> **Supporting data**: McKinsey research shows that remote teams take 40% longer to make decisions than colocated teams on average.
>
> **Typical case**: One technology company found that its product-iteration cycle grew from two weeks to four after moving to remote work, primarily because of insufficient synchronous communication.
>
> **Recommendation**: Companies should introduce structured synchronous-collaboration tools, schedule fixed synchronization windows, and establish rapid decision-making mechanisms.

## Conclusion
**Requirement**: Summarize the core point and provide recommended next steps.

### Core Summary
Summarize the article's central point and key insights in two or three sentences.

**Example**:
The remote-work productivity drain is not a technology problem but a structural problem in collaboration mechanisms. Companies must shift from optimizing the physical environment to redesigning the operating mechanisms.

### Recommended Actions
Provide three to five specific, actionable recommendations.

**Example**:
1. **Assess the current state**: Conduct a comprehensive review of team collaboration mechanisms and identify bottlenecks.
2. **Establish mechanisms**: Introduce structured synchronous-collaboration tools and processes.
3. **Build capability**: Train the team in remote-collaboration skills.
4. **Continuously improve**: Establish feedback mechanisms and iterate on collaboration practices.

### Outlook (Optional)
Offer a judgment or prediction about future trends.

**Example**:
As digital collaboration tools mature, structured collaboration will become the new standard for remote work, delivering both flexibility and efficiency.

## Structure Checklist
- [ ] The title is compelling and descriptive.
- [ ] The introduction uses the complete SCQA framework.
- [ ] Each argument has a clear subheading and topic sentence.
- [ ] Each argument includes the phenomenon, analysis, evidence, and recommendation.
- [ ] The arguments are logically organized and do not overlap.
- [ ] The arguments fully support the core conclusion.
- [ ] The conclusion summarizes the core point and provides recommended actions.
- [ ] The complete logic chain is sound and persuasive.
